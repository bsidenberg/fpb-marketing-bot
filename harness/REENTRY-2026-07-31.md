## 4. The pivot — what you actually asked for

You want a tool you can call on to optimize your ads. That is **not** what the remaining queue builds. S-08A.1a.2, S-04B.2, S-08B, S-09/S-09B all exist to let Prime *act on your account without you*. That's the expensive part, and it's where the entire A14/A15/A17/A19 exploit surface lives.

**The exploit surface exists because a model's proposal feeds a scoring function that gates automated execution.** Take the execution away and it collapses. A wrong recommendation to a human costs five minutes of judgment. The same recommendation to an executor costs money.

So: ship **Advisor Mode** — a read-only Prime that fetches honestly, analyses, and hands you a ranked list you act on yourself in Google Ads. Three sessions, not fifteen.

### S-ADV-1 · read-only posture, enforced server-side
A mode where no write action can be staged at all — enforced in code, not by prompt instruction. Not "the model is told not to"; the staging path is absent. This is what lets you ship without the A14 chain resolved, because there is nothing for an inflated score to unlock.
*Acceptance: an adversarial test that proposes a maximally-scored write action in advisor mode and gets structurally refused.*

### S-ADV-2 · kill the fabrication root cause
`fpb.js:191` tells the model to reuse earlier data without re-requesting it. `:201`/`:207` forbid inventing terms or reconstructing from memory. Rows aren't carried forward — so on a staging turn, **fabrication is the compliant answer.** That's the 7/28 field failure, and it's prompt design, not model misbehaviour. Either carry rows forward in context or force a re-fetch. Never both instructions at once.
*Last night's S-07f.0 blocker-2 fix already landed part of this: `fetchSearchTerms` failures now surface instead of failing silent in `chat.js`.*
*Acceptance: the 7/28 transcript replayed as an eval, fabrication impossible rather than discouraged.*

### S-ADV-3 · ledger + capability disclosure (was S-07h, D-10)
Render what was actually fetched — row counts, fetch timestamps, which tools ran — in the UI, **outside the model's context** so it cannot be narrated. Right now the model describes its own tool calls in prose and you have no independent view. That's what let italic `*Fetching search term reports...*` pass for a fetch.
*Acceptance: you can look at a panel and know whether a fetch happened, without asking.*

**Then you have your tool.** It reads your account honestly, tells you where the waste is, shows you its evidence, and cannot touch anything. You make the changes. When the autonomy work eventually lands, it plugs into a system you already trust.
